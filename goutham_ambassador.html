01 — Who I Am

Builder. Leader. Connector.

I'm a Product Engineer at Vobiz AI, where we're building telephony infrastructure for the next generation of voice AI agents. My work spans AI integration, internal automation, product scaling, and driving the company's US go-to-market — operating at the intersection of deep technology and business strategy.


Beyond the product work, I'm a global IEEE leader — recognised as an Outstanding Volunteer by the IEEE Asia-Pacific Region (R10) for my contributions across the IEEE ecosystem. I led the student branch at GEC Barton Hill to become the first and only university in the world to win every award offered by the IEEE MGA Global Students Department — a record that stands to this day. I currently serve in an industry-focused leadership capacity at IEEE Kerala Section, driving communities around innovation and entrepreneurship. Across parts of Kerala and Bangalore, I've built and led product and community initiatives that connect students, founders, and industry.


I'm also a 2× founder, with a track record of building AI-powered products that reach real users across multiple countries. What drives me is simple: I want to be in the rooms where things get built — and make sure the right people are in those rooms with me.
